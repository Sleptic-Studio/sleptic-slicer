## What you may do without asking

- **Use the software.** You may run Official Releases, and builds you make
  for your own private use as the license allows. Those builds may keep the
  names and logo, because no one else receives them.
- **Contribute.** You may open issues, send pull requests and discuss the
  project under its name.
- **Refer to the project truthfully.** You may write about it, review it,
  teach it, link to it, and say your product is "compatible with Sleptic
  Slicer", as long as you don't suggest Sleptic made or endorses your work.

## Official releases

An **Official Release** is a build, installer, package or source archive
that Sleptic publishes through an Official Channel and identifies as a
release; a copy counts only if exactly as published. Official Channels are
the official repository and its Releases page, Sleptic's website, and any
other channel Sleptic names as official in a Written Authorization or on
the official repository, as defined in license sections 1.5 and 1.6.
Unauthorized redistribution does not change a release's origin or make
that distribution authorized.
